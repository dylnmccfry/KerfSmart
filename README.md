# KerfSmart
KerfSmart Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9/30/26

KerfSmart does not collect, store or share any personal information.

No accounts. You never sign in or create an account.
No data collection. The app does not send any data to us or to anyone else. It has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
On-device storage only. Your settings, calculator history and cut lists are saved on your device so they are there next time you open the app. They are removed when you delete the app.
Sharing is your choice. When you print or share a cut list, it goes through the standard iOS print or share sheet to the app or printer you pick. KerfSmart does not see or keep a copy.
Because no data is collected, the app is suitable for all ages.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
