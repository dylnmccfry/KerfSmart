Built the way you measure

Tape-measure calculator

Add, subtract, multiply and divide lengths. Round to anything from 1/2" to 1/64", with conversions, square feet, board feet and full metric mode.

Plywood layouts

Parts laid out on the fewest sheets with straight-through cuts, numbered cut lines, step-by-step instructions and grain lock. Print or share as a PDF.

Lumber and length cut lists

Circular saw guide

Carpentry tools

Safety notice and disclaimer

KerfSmart does the math. You make the call before the blade touches wood.

Check every measurement before you cut. KerfSmart's results are estimates for planning. They are not engineering, architectural or building-code advice. For stairs, rafters and any structural work, check your local building code and use a qualified professional.

Effective date: October 4, 2026

Check before you cut

Results are only as accurate as what you enter. Rounding to the fraction you choose, the saw kerf and offsets you set, and real-world material (lumber that is warped, oversized or undersized) all affect the outcome. Verify measurements on your actual material, and make a test cut on scrap when it matters.

Framing and structural work

Stair, rafter, stud layout and other framing results are estimates for planning only. They are not engineering, architectural, inspection or code-compliance advice. The stair limits shown by default follow common U.S. residential guidelines and may not match your local code. Check with your local building department, get any required permits, and have structural work designed or inspected by a qualified professional.

Tool safety

Woodworking and construction tools can cause serious injury. Follow your tool manufacturer's instructions, keep guards and safety features working, wear eye and hearing protection, support your work properly, and never work when tired or impaired. You are solely responsible for how you use tools and materials.

Cost and material estimates

Board foot, cost and cut list results are estimates. Buy extra material to allow for defects and mistakes.

Set up your saw

In Settings, enter your blade kerf (usually 1/8") and your base plate offsets. Clamp a straightedge, make a cut, and measure from the straightedge to the cut edge to find each offset.

Projects not syncing?

Make sure you are signed in to iCloud and that KerfSmart is turned on under iOS Settings > [your name] > iCloud. Projects sync when both devices are online.
